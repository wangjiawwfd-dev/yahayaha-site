YahaYaha Support

Need help with YahaYaha? This page provides basic support and account information.

Account Help

Registered users can log out from the Home screen without deleting their account. To permanently delete an account, open Settings and choose Delete Account.

Guest Accounts

Guest users can leave their guest session from the Home screen. Leaving a guest session permanently deletes that guest account and its associated YahaYaha account data.

Blocking and Reporting

If another user is bothering you, you can block that user from chat or your Friends list. You can also report a user or a specific chat message.

Gameplay Issues

If a match does not behave as expected, restarting the app may resolve temporary connection issues. Online play requires an internet connection.
