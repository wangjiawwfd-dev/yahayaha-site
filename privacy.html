Privacy Policy for YahaYaha

Last updated: October 15, 2026

YahaYaha is a three-player strategy game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

YahaYaha may collect and store the following information:

  • Account identifiers and user IDs
  • Username, nickname, and profile information
  • Gameplay information such as scores, game state, and match participation
  • Friend relationships and private-room invitations
  • Messages sent through the in-app friend chat
  • Reports and block relationships used for user safety

Guest Accounts

YahaYaha allows users to continue as a guest. Guest accounts use an anonymous Firebase Authentication identifier. If a guest chooses to leave the guest session, the guest account and associated YahaYaha account data are deleted.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate and improve YahaYaha, including to:

  • Provide online matchmaking and multiplayer gameplay
  • Maintain player profiles, scores, skins, and game state
  • Provide friends, chat, and private-room features
  • Support blocking, reporting, and account safety features
  • Provide account deletion and account management

Third-Party Services

YahaYaha uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions, to provide account, multiplayer, and data storage functionality.

Advertising and Tracking

YahaYaha does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use advertising tracking or IDFA for behavioral advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase.

User Content and Safety

Registered users may communicate through private friend chat. YahaYaha provides tools to block users and report users or messages. Reported content may be retained for safety and moderation purposes.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion directly inside the app from Settings > Delete Account. Registered accounts and guest accounts can both be deleted.

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, abuse reporting, legal obligations, or protection of other users.

Data Retention

Account and gameplay information is retained while needed to provide the service. When an account is deleted, associated account data is deleted or disconnected where appropriate, while certain game history or safety reports may be retained when necessary.

Children

YahaYaha is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as YahaYaha changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, please visit the YahaYaha Support page.
